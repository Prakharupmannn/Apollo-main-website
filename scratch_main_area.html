What is EBUS (Endobronchial Ultrasound)?

EBUS (Endobronchial Ultrasound) is a minimally invasive diagnostic procedure that uses ultrasound through the bronchial airways to evaluate lymph nodes and surrounding lung structures.

It helps diagnose:

  • Lung cancer
  • Enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes
  • Tuberculosis (TB)
  • Sarcoidosis
  • Complex pulmonary infections

EBUS enables accurate biopsy and tissue sampling without open surgery.
